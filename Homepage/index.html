About IDR

Industry-led.
Academically Grounded.

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute purpose-built for digital, cyber and technology risk roles in regulated, high-consequence environments. We exist at the intersection of practitioner expertise and academic rigour — producing professionals ready to deploy from day one.

Through a strategic partnership with a leading UK university, IDR blends evidence-based academic insight with live project work and employer-embedded placements. Our graduates and associates don't just understand risk theory — they have managed it in the field, under pressure, across sectors that cannot afford to fail.

NIST ISO 27001 NIS2 AI Governance Cyber Risk DORA

"We don't just train people to pass exams. We train people to manage the risks that keep boards awake at night — then we place them where it matters most."

Founded by practitioners with backgrounds spanning financial services, critical national infrastructure and government, IDR brings real-world credibility to every programme it designs.

Our UK university partnership ensures every curriculum is benchmarked against the latest academic research in digital risk, AI governance and technology resilience — keeping IDR graduates ahead of the regulatory curve.
