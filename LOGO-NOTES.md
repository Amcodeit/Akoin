# IDR Logo Design Notes

## Design Overview

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) logo uses an **isometric 3D cube** as its primary symbol. This geometric form was chosen to represent **structure, multi-dimensional risk analysis, and organisational resilience** — core principles of the institute's mission. The cube's three visible faces suggest the interconnected nature of digital, cyber, and technology risk domains.

## Colour Palette

| Colour | Hex Code | Usage |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Orange (Primary) | `#F05E1C` / `#FF6B1A` | Action, alertness, energy |
| Black | `#0D0D0D` | Authority, technology, sophistication |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clarity, contrast, accessibility |

**Orange** was selected as the primary accent colour for its associations with **caution, alertness, and proactive action** — aligning with IDR's focus on risk management and early intervention. It avoids the aggression of red while maintaining urgency.

**Black** anchors the palette with **authority and technical credibility**, positioning IDR as a serious, professional institution. The near-black (#0D0D0D) provides depth without harshness.

## Typography

The wordmark uses **Barlow Condensed** (700 weight) for the "IDR" abbreviation, paired with **Barlow** (400 weight) for the full name "Institute of Digital Risk". This combination delivers a **modern, engineering-inspired aesthetic** that balances impact with readability — suitable for both digital interfaces and print applications.

## Symbolism

The isometric cube serves multiple symbolic functions:

1. **Structure & Framework** — Reflects IDR's work with governance frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001, NIS2)
2. **Multi-Dimensional Risk** — Three faces represent digital, cyber, and technology risk domains
3. **Building Blocks** — Suggests education, capability building, and foundation-laying
4. **Resilience** — The stable, grounded form conveys robustness and dependability

The subtle diagonal accent line on the cube's right face hints at **risk vectors and forward momentum**, reinforcing the institute's proactive, future-focused approach.

## Accessibility

The orange-on-black palette achieves a contrast ratio of approximately **4.8:1** for large text and graphic elements, meeting WCAG AA standards. For small body text, lighter grays (#D4D4D4) are used to ensure readability against dark backgrounds.
